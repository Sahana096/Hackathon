# 🎬 60-Second Video Demo Script: RecallMeet

> **Use this exact script for recording your hackathon demo video (Loom/YouTube) or presenting live to judges.**

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:00 – 0:12 | The Hook & Problem
- **On Screen:** Open the Dashboard at [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173).
- **Spoken Audio:**  
  *"Every sales rep and account manager knows the pain of meeting amnesia. You're heading into your 5th call with a key client, but today's AI chatbots treat every meeting like day one. Meet RecallMeet, the AI pre-meeting intelligence agent powered by Vectorize Hindsight."*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:12 – 0:25 | The Failure of Amnesic AI (Memory OFF)
- **On Screen:** 
  1. Toggle **Memory OFF** in the top navbar.
  2. Click the **"Prepare Me for This Meeting"** button.
- **Spoken Audio:**  
  *"Watch what happens when memory is disabled. Normal AI gives us generic boilerplate: 'Review customer needs', 'Discuss pricing', 'What are your goals for this quarter?'. It has no idea Sarah met with Acme Technologies 4 times before, that annual billing was already agreed on, or that Priya requested security docs."*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:25 – 0:42 | The WOW Moment (Memory ON)
- **On Screen:** 
  1. Toggle **Memory ON** in the navbar.
  2. Click **"Prepare Me for This Meeting"**.
  3. Scroll through the synthesized output:
     - The **7 Hindsight Memory Checklist Items**
     - The **⚠️ Open Commitments Alert**
     - The **Tactical Questions**
- **Spoken Audio:**  
  *"Now, let's flip Memory ON with Hindsight. Look at the immediate transformation! Hindsight instantly recalls 12 cross-meeting memories across 4 memory networks: World, Experiences, Observations, and Opinions. It alerts Sarah: ⚠️ Priya's SOC2 security review is still pending, and John is waiting for the executable contract with annual billing terms. Same meeting, but an executive superpower briefing."*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:42 – 0:54 | Dynamic Ingestion & Live Learning
- **On Screen:** 
  1. Click **"+ Ingest Note"** in the top navbar.
  2. Click **"⚡ Scenario A: Priya approves SOC2"**.
  3. Click **"Retain to Hindsight (Ingest & Reflect)"**.
  4. Watch confetti fire and the briefing automatically refresh to show the security blocker cleared!
- **Spoken Audio:**  
  *"And RecallMeet truly learns in real-time. Let's ingest a quick note: 'Priya officially approved SOC2 compliance'. Watch Hindsight retain, extract, and reflect. Instantly, the blocker vanishes, and our briefing questions adapt to close the contract!"*

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp: 0:54 – 1:00 | Conclusion & Impact
- **On Screen:** Show the **Demo Mode** or **Memory Networks** view.
- **Spoken Audio:**  
  *"Stateless chatbots forget. RecallMeet remembers, reasons, and adapts using Hindsight. Thanks for watching!"*
