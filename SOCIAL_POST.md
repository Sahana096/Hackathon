🚀 Excited to unveil **RecallMeet** — our hackathon project for the **Vectorize Hindsight Hackathon**! 🧠

Every sales rep and account manager knows the pain of **meeting amnesia**. You are heading into your 5th meeting with an enterprise prospect, but modern AI chatbots treat every call like day one. They generate generic discovery fluff ("What are your goals?") because they have zero persistent memory across calls.

We built **RecallMeet** to change that using **Vectorize Hindsight**.

Instead of treating memory as a dump of vector embeddings, RecallMeet leverages Hindsight’s 4 memory networks:
🌍 **World Bank:** Objective facts & entity graph (John has signing authority, Competitor X cited)  
📜 **Experiences Bank:** Episodic meeting logs & promises made  
🔬 **Observations Bank:** Inferred patterns & blockers (<30 day rollout, pending security review)  
💭 **Opinions Bank:** Customer preferences with confidence scores (Annual billing: 98% confidence)

Toggle Memory ON ➡️ Executive briefing alerting Sarah on Priya’s pending SOC2 security review and John’s executable contract terms.
